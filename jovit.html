ABOUT US

Welcome to Jovit Mall! Where shopping feels Like Home.

Jovit Mall is a vibrant, modern shopping and lifestyle destination located in the heart of the city. Designed as a complete family-friendly experience, Jovit Mall brings together fashion, dining, entertainment, and convenience in one beautiful space.

OUR STORY

Established in January 19,2025, Jovit Mall was created with a simple vision: to build a welcoming environment where people love to spend their time. What started as a shopping center has grown into a beloved community hub where families, friends, and individuals come to shop, eat, relax, and create beautiful memories.

What Makes Jovit Mall Special

  • Curated Shopping: A perfect mix of local favorites and international brands across fashion, beauty, electronics, home & lifestyle, and more.
  • Diverse Dining: From quick delicious bites at the food court to upscale restaurants and cozy cafés — something to satisfy every craving.
  • Entertainment & Fun: Children’s play area, gaming zones, and regular family events throughout the year.
  • comfort & Convenience: Ample parking, free Wi-Fi, clean restrooms, and attentive customer service.

At Jovit Mall, we believe shopping should be more than just buying things — it should be an enjoyable experience. That’s why we continuously update our stores, host exciting events, and maintain high standards of cleanliness and safety. Whether you’re dropping in for a quick purchase, enjoying a family day out, or meeting friends for coffee, we are committed to making every visit to Jovit Mall pleasant and memorable.
